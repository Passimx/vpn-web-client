1. Terms and Definitions

The following terms are used in this Privacy Policy:

  • Service — the PassimX technology platform providing the User with access to the VPN service and related functionality.
  • Operator — the person or entity administering and managing the PassimX Service.
  • User — an individual using the Service.
  • VPN Connection — a network connection between the User and PassimX infrastructure used to access the Internet.
  • Configuration — technical connection parameters provided to the User for using the VPN service.
  • Balance — an internal accounting value representing the User's funds within the Service and not constituting a bank account or electronic money.

2. General Provisions

2.1. This Privacy Policy applies to all data that the Operator receives and processes in connection with the User's use of the Service.

2.2. By using the Service, the User acknowledges this Privacy Policy and agrees to its terms to the extent provided for by applicable law.

2.3. The Operator aims to process only the data necessary for the operation of the Service, security, payment processing, management of the internal balance, and provision of technical support.

The Operator does not maintain a log of the websites visited by the User and does not create a history of specific domains or URLs visited by the User solely on the basis of their use of the VPN service.

3. Data We Process

3.1. Depending on the functionality used, the Operator may process the following categories of data:

  • User identifier within the system;
  • tg_id and username — when using the PassimX Telegram bot;
  • technical identifiers and parameters of the VPN Configuration;
  • information about the User's plan, subscription, and subscription period;
  • history of deposits to the internal Balance;
  • history of transactions associated with the internal Balance, including deposits, deductions, credits, conversions, and other transactions;
  • information necessary to verify and account for payments;
  • VPN traffic data processed as part of the operation of the VPN infrastructure;
  • technical events necessary to ensure the operation and security of the Service.

3.2. The Operator does not request passport details, document photographs, private correspondence, or other excessive personal data unless necessary.

3.3. The User independently determines what additional information to provide to the Operator when contacting customer support.

4. VPN Traffic Data

4.1. In order to provide the VPN service, User network traffic passes through the PassimX infrastructure.

4.2. The Operator stores VPN traffic data to the extent necessary for the operation of the Service, ensuring its security, diagnosing technical issues, and preventing abuse.

4.3. At the same time, the Operator does not maintain a history of websites visited. In particular, the Service does not create a dedicated history of the websites, domains, or URLs accessed by the User through the VPN.

4.4. The absence of a website browsing history means that the Operator does not use a history of visited websites as a separate database or User profile.

A VPN service should not be considered a guarantee of absolute anonymity. The use of third-party applications, websites, payment systems, and other services may result in such parties independently collecting information about the User.

5. Payments and Internal Balance

5.1. In order to provide paid services, the Operator stores the history of deposits to the User's internal Balance.

5.2. The Operator also stores information about all transactions associated with the internal Balance, including deposits, deductions, credits, refunds, conversions, and other changes to the Balance.

5.3. Transaction history is used to correctly maintain the Balance, verify completed transactions, provide information to the User, resolve disputes, and operate paid features of the Service.

5.4. When a third-party payment provider is used, payment data may also be processed by the respective payment provider in accordance with its own privacy policy and rules.

5.5. The Operator is not required to store full bank card details or other payment instrument information if such data is processed directly by a third-party payment provider.

6. Purposes of Data Processing

6.1. User data is processed for the following purposes:

  • providing access to the VPN service;
  • creating and managing VPN Configurations;
  • managing subscriptions and plans;
  • processing and verifying payments;
  • managing the User's internal Balance;
  • maintaining transaction history;
  • converting funds within the Service;
  • ensuring infrastructure security;
  • detecting and preventing abuse;
  • diagnosing technical issues and maintaining the Service;
  • processing User requests submitted to customer support;
  • complying with applicable legal requirements.

6.2. The Operator does not use website browsing history to create advertising profiles of Users.

7. Data Storage

7.1. Data is stored for the period necessary to achieve the purposes of processing, operate the Service, maintain payment and transaction history, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

7.2. Deposit and internal Balance transaction history may be retained after the User stops using the Service for the period necessary to verify financial transactions, resolve disputes, maintain accounting and other mandatory records, if such retention is required by applicable law.

7.3. Data may be deleted or anonymized once it is no longer necessary to retain it, unless further retention is required by law or necessary to protect the Operator's legitimate interests.

The Operator does not store a separate history of websites visited by the User, unless otherwise expressly required by technical or legal requirements applicable to a particular situation.

8. Disclosure to Third Parties

8.1. The Operator does not sell Users' personal data to third parties.

8.2. Data may be disclosed to third parties only to the extent necessary for the operation of the Service or where required by applicable law.

8.3. Such third parties may include:

  • payment providers and acquiring organizations — for processing and verifying payments;
  • server and network infrastructure providers — for operating the VPN service and related systems;
  • software and technical service providers — to the extent necessary for the operation of the Service;
  • government authorities and other authorized persons — where disclosure is required by applicable law.

8.4. When disclosing data, the Operator seeks to limit the amount of information to what is necessary for the relevant purpose.

9. Data Security

9.1. The Operator takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect processed data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, destruction, and other unlawful actions.

9.2. Access to data is provided only to the infrastructure components and persons who require such access to operate the Service and perform the relevant functions.

9.3. Despite the security measures implemented, no method of storing or transmitting data over the Internet can guarantee absolute protection of information.

10. User Rights

10.1. The User may contact customer support regarding the processing of their data and request information to the extent provided by applicable law.

10.2. The User may request correction of inaccurate data, where technically possible and provided that such correction does not conflict with applicable legal requirements.

10.3. The User may request deletion of their data if its further retention is not required for the performance of obligations, verification of payments, maintenance of transaction history, protection of the Operator's rights, or compliance with applicable law.

10.4. For privacy-related requests, the User may contact PassimX support: Telegram Support.

Deletion of certain data may not be possible if its retention is necessary to verify financial transactions, fulfill obligations, or comply with legal requirements.

11. Changes to this Policy

11.1. The Operator may periodically update this Privacy Policy in connection with changes to the Service functionality, data processing methods, security requirements, or applicable law.

11.2. The new version of the Privacy Policy becomes effective upon publication, unless otherwise stated in the new version.

11.3. The date of the current version is indicated at the top of this page.

12. Final Provisions

12.1. This Privacy Policy forms an integral part of the terms governing the use of the PassimX Service with respect to the processing of data.

12.2. If any provision of this Policy is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

12.3. The current version of this Policy is always available at: https://vpn-client.passimx.com/info/en/privacy-policy.html.
